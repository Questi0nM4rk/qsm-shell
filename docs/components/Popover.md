# Popover

Anchored floating content that flips to the side with the most room and clamps into bounds; it places and reports, and the surface decides open state.
states: open and closed with an opacity and offset settle between them (`Motion.medium` in, `Motion.exit` out, the offset is `space2` from the side it sits on); the shown `side` follows fit, not the request; placement lands on the device grid.
actions: `toggle()` requests open or dismiss by state; `openRequested` and `dismissRequested` report intent, Escape reports dismissal, `hovered` reports the pointer over the anchor or the popover; open state never changes inside.
pointer: a hover trigger opens after `openDelay` (`Motion.hoverIntent`) and closes `closeDelay` (`Motion.hoverGrace`) after the pointer leaves the anchor, the panel and the strip between them (a listener across the gap, so a pointer that lingers there keeps it open), so the panel can be entered; a press trigger toggles on the anchor; wheel passes through.
keyboard: a press trigger follows the anchor's own `clicked` (a button's Space and Return) and taps an anchor that has none; a hover trigger also asks at once when a `T.Control` anchor has keyboard focus; Escape anywhere in the window reports dismissal while open (a child that needs Escape accepts it in `Keys.onShortcutOverride`); a close with the focus inside the panel gives it back to the anchor; the content keeps its own tab stops.
screen reader: no role of its own; the anchored content names itself.
size: content size rounded up to the device grid, a fixed anchor `gap` (`space2`); placement resamples every frame while open so anchor and bounds moves are followed.
tokens: space2 and the Motion delay, hoverGrace and easing set; no local colours or measurements.
motion: emphasized entry and exit curve on opacity and offset; reduced motion removes the offset and shortens the opacity duration.
expected: shell Workbench → Popover: an output button whose detail opens beside it; placement, alignment, trigger and room are controls; with the anchor against an edge the panel flips; Tab reaches the action in the panel and Escape returns to the button.
not designed: modal takeover, focus trapping or dismissal policy; use Dialog, Rollout or the host for those. A panel taller than its bounds is clamped to the bounds' start and does not scroll.

Measured (default theme, 1.25 scale, headless window): the panel sits at x 304.8 (381 device px), 8 below the anchor with the centres 0 px apart; the anchor is 132 by 40 and the panel's action 114 by 32 (24 px floor); all four sides and the tight flips land on the side with room (flow popover-placement); a pointer resting 800 ms in the gap keeps the panel open, and the flow reads `hovered` there once the panel has settled (flow popover-hover); Space and Return on a button anchor open a press popover and a keyboard-focused anchor opens a hover popover (flows popover-keyboard, popover-hover); open reaches its colour in about 146 ms and the exit in about 162 ms of its 208 ms at 60 fps.
