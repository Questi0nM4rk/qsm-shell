# NotificationCard

A person receiving a notification reads its source, headline and message, then opens the source's default action or dismisses it. The host owns the record, the availability of every action and every consequence; the card renders and reports intent.
states: rest, hover (the body lifts a tone; a popup row also opens, a list row does not), press (body tone; title accent on a boxless row), focus (keyboard-only ring outside the body), disabled (`Style.disabled` on every role, input withdrawn), selected (accent tint at `opacitySelected`, a tick on a boxless row), unread (accent dot), archived (age from `archivedAt`, no action row), empty body (48 px header only), long body (popup: five-line viewport with an edge fade, list: full height)
look: `bodied` (default) is a `surface` body of `bodyRadius` with a slight shadow; false is a boxless row with a divider. Urgency is the glyph's shape first and its colour second: critical `alert-triangle` in `danger`, normal `bell`, low `info` in `textMuted`; the moon is do-not-disturb's alone. A swatch or image takes the glyph's slot and an urgent entry keeps its glyph as a 16 px badge on a disc of the card's own colour on the slot's corner
layout: 12 px on all four sides; the 24 px lead column, then the text at `Style.notificationTextColumn` (48); headline 24 tall (app name capped at a third of it, unread dot, summary, then the age); the age and the dismiss button share one trailing slot, so nothing moves when the pointer arrives; heights 48 (no body), 72 / 92 (one / two preview lines), 152 (popup expanded, long body), whole body plus 14 px of actions (list expanded)
actions: `dismiss()` (the dismiss button, the middle click, a 40 % swipe, Delete, Backspace or Escape on the focused card, the screen reader's press), `activatePrimary()` (the body tap, Enter, Space, the screen reader) only when the host says the default exists, `activateAction(id)` (a label click, the host's cursor). One tap is routed by where it lands: lead column opens a list row (`expandRequested`), dismiss button dismisses, the rest is the card's action (`primaryTriggered`, else `activated` for an unread row). The card never calls a service or mutates `entry`
pointer: hover is read on the card itself, so it holds over the lead column and the dismiss button; popup hover opens the row, list hover does not (the wheel belongs to the list); the swipe follows the pointer one to one and eases home on release; the wheel scrolls an expanded popup body and passes through at its ends
keyboard: a Tab stop only while a primary action exists and the host grants focus; a popup window without keyboard focus has no native keyboard route
screen reader: Button named `<action>: <summary>` when primary is available, else static text `<app>: <summary>`; "Unread." prefix; the body is the description once; the dismiss button is a named Button
size: fixed `Style.notificationWidth`; long content wraps, scrolls or elides and never grows the card past the viewport
tokens: notification width, collapsed and preview heights, body viewport (rounded to whole lines), text column, badge dot, icon scale, space scale, radiusBody, card shadow, `Surface` ladder, Motion
motion: entrance settle, height `Motion.fast`, preview/body cross-fade, spring-back; none on a held drag
expected: a held card is where the pointer is; a hover that has nothing to open still lifts the body; every pointer and key route reports through the same function; a retiring row refuses input and keeps its paint
not designed: lifetime, protocol lookup, grouping, dismissal policy, host geometry
