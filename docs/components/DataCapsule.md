# DataCapsule

An auxiliary data container for a quick reading without leaving the application. It is not the Capsule button or a Rollout: no edge joins, translation, deformation, services or window policy. A host places it above fullscreen when appropriate.
slot: caller content in one clipped slot (`contentItem`, a `FocusScope`); the host centres the complete content group in it and owns the infoblock. Orientation chooses the composition, never the text's rotation: horizontal is a centred text group, vertical a glyph with its reading.
size: `Style.size("DataCapsule", size)` is the cross axis (sm 32, md 64, lg 96) and `preferredLength` the other, both scaled by `sizeScale`; padding is `space3` scaled onto the rhythm; radius is `rolloutRadius`, which Qt clamps to the body's short side. The host places it and sets the footprint.
states: shown or hidden by the host (`shown`); `timeout <= 0` is persistent, a positive one asks the host once through `timeoutRequested`; `hoverStay` pauses it under the pointer and keyboard focus (never a click's focus) holds it; `interactive: false` is report-only, legible and dead; `enabled: false` is unavailable and refuses every route, the host dims its own copy by colour with `Style.disabled`. Hover lifts the band body one step and press two, with no growth or positional motion; the body is `surfaceColor` (`Style.band`) with a floating shadow (`elevated`, removed by `Style.reducedEffects` and never part of the input footprint).
actions: `activate()` emits `activated` only when shown, enabled and interactive, and the body click, Return, Enter, Space and the exposed action all call it; a held key asks once; nested controls keep their own action and the empty body is the whole-capsule action; `restart()` refreshes the timeout, `reset()` cancels it without touching `shown`.
facts: `pointerHovered`, `keepOpen`, `present`, `animating`, `acceptsInput` and `remainingTime()` are what a window host needs; a fading-out capsule must not keep an input mask.
screen reader: a button (with `actionLabel` as its description and the press action) when interactive, a grouping otherwise, named by `accessibleName`; an interactive capsule without one warns.
motion: opacity only, a reversible fade (`Motion.medium` in, `Motion.micro` out); reduced motion shortens it.
tokens: Surface, StateLayer, `Style.band`, `rolloutRadius`, the floating shadow roles, the rhythm and Motion.
measured (rendered, scale 1.25): text 8.5:1 for the reading and 13.7:1 for the title on the rest body, 6.0 and 9.8 on hover, 4.7 and 7.6 on press; the hover zone is exactly the body (380 by 64); keyboard focus rings the body; the unit's copy dims by colour when disabled.
not designed: a service, a window policy, an edge join or a Rollout's deformation.
