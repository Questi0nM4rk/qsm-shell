# Slider

states: rest hover(thumb grows one `space1` step per side in whole device pixels at every size, overflowing the control and never added to its layout size; a pill grows along its length only, twice as far) press(growth kept, shadow lift doubles) focus(ring around the track at a spaceTight gap, concentric, under the thumbs, never around the label) disabled(every painted role through Style.disabled, still reads)
range: one type, one or two thumbs; range: true paints the low thumb (lowValue) and the fill runs between the thumbs, which never cross (Qt keeps low <= high); keyboard, wheel and actions step the last thumb a pointer grabbed, the high one until then; the high thumb alone carries thumbIcon/thumbText
actions: Increase Decrease Minimum Maximum (on the active thumb), Activate label (`activate-label`) when its label is enabled, actionable and nonempty; each action has a stable objectName. `activateLabel(): bool` emits `labelClicked` without changing host state.
size: `size` is a preset name resolved by Style.size("Slider", name) into thumbSize (thickness/thumbSize stay the API); s = preset / md; track = rhythm(two thirds of the preset) above the sm preset, a space1 hairline at or below it (under 0.7 of the thumb the fill's rounded end stays hidden behind the thumb, geometry not taste; a track thicker than its thumb runs the fill on to a full round cap concentric with the thumb, so a ring of fill colour surrounds it and no straight edge shows, and at either bound the ring is the track's end cap); hover growth is one fixed `space1` step (25%, 17% and 10% of the thumb at sm, md and lg: the bigger the instance, the calmer), label gap rhythm(space2·s), label text/glyph through ControlLabel.sizeScale. Main axis sliderLength / sliderLengthVertical plus the label, in whole pixels; cross axis at least Control `xs` and the thumb; growth and the shadow drop overflow it by at most one growth step plus the lift (doubled while pressed)
thumb: thumbStyle circle (rides on the track, carries thumbIcon or thumbText) | pill (space1 bar standing space2 proud of the track, in a space1 gap whose cut ends are radiusXs/2, Material stop dot at the far end, carries nothing); the thumb's own box, the one a hover meets, is at least the Control `xs` floor each way with the paint centred in it (a 4 px pill answers a 24 px zone); a reading is `textSm` in an md thumb and steps up the type scale with the thumb (12, 18 at lg); thumbIconSize sets the glyph edge (default: the thumb less space2 per side, snapped by Style.iconNear); grabThumb (default off) makes a press on the thumb a grab that moves nothing and a drag then keeps the pointer's distance from the thumb's centre, where off it jumps the value to the pointer like a press anywhere; the thumb carries its own drag handler, so a host's TapHandler or other pointer handler on `second.handle` does not stop the drag; a circle's travel is never shorter than the track is thick
label: ControlLabel (the element Field uses above a text box) with labelText and/or labelIcon (label role, iconSm) at labelPosition above|below|left|right, whatever the orientation; it takes padding on its side so the track never moves for it. labelClickable makes it the slider's one action surface (StateLayer, hit box at least targetFloor, hand cursor) and the host maps the click in onLabelClicked; off, it is a caption and takes no pointer. `labelAccessibleName` defaults to labelText for icon-only labels. Readonly `labelItem` exposes that same actual ControlLabel as a native composition target. A long label widens the natural size until ControlLabel has its width ceiling (BACKLOG).
accessibility: `accessibleName` (default labelText) names the slider (role Slider) and its thumbs; a range's thumbs read `<name> minimum` / `<name> maximum`. The thumb holds the keyboard focus, so it carries value, from, to and stepSize (its node's) and the increase and decrease actions: that is what Qt's accessibility reads for a slider. `labelAccessibleName` names only the label's action
pointer: with one thumb, every press anywhere in the control's cross band (not only the track's thickness) takes one path, `pointerPath`; the label is outside it
wheel: `wheelUnits > 0` is the host's opt-in; a vertical wheel drives either orientation (a mouse has no other axis), a sideways wheel only a horizontal slider; a host that wants the scroller under the slider leaves it 0
colours: fill (default primary, the active part of a track) trackColor thumbColor thumbContentColor are the host's; the component never recolours a part for a state (the thumb is the lightest role, so growth and the lift are its hover and press cues)
tokens: sliderStep sliderKeyboardUnits sliderPageUnits sliderLength sliderLengthVertical sliderThumbLift space1 space2 iconXs iconSm iconLg targetFloor the Control size table primary overlay text textOnPrimary textMuted textSm weightBold Style.disabled
motion: fast+standardEasing on the painted position, only when the change did not come from a gesture; micro on thumb growth and colour; press on the shadow lift. Reduced motion removes the position animation and shortens the rest
events: moved(gesture in progress) committed(gesture finished, a held key that lost focus, availability or visibility included; committedValue and committedLowValue are where the last gesture left the values)
keyboard: focused track/thumb handles its axis arrows, Page/Home/End and shared decrease/increase keys from Interaction (N/O by default). `decreaseKey`/`increaseKey` override them per instance; zero disables a custom binding and equal custom bindings are ignored. Ctrl/Alt/Meta custom combinations and focused label actions are not level commands. Text entry elsewhere stays text entry. Repeats nudge; real release commits once; focus/availability loss clears held-key state. Horizontal inline use maps N/left to decrease and O/right to increase in the normal LTR layout; the existing mirrored arrow semantics remain unchanged.
host keys: `keyPressed(event)` exposes the live event before value/template handling; accepting it claims navigation or editor exit while all other keys retain the shared Slider route. It is the same interception contract as TextField, not a separate keyboard implementation.
not designed: end slots (the label is the one thing beside a slider), ticks, wavy fill, value tooltip

## Measured

Slot at scale 1.25. Hover growth (thumb at rest to hovered): sm 16 to 24, md 24 to 32, lg 40 to 48 (lg was 40 to 56). A 4 px pill answers hover within 12 px of its centre (24 px zone, measured with `drive hitmap`; it answered within about 2 px). The track starts on a whole pixel (x 163, it was 163.48 behind a fractional label width). A thumb reading at lg is 18 px and 12 px at md (both were 10). Fill on track 5.19:1, thumb on track 8.02:1, thumb on fill 1.54:1 with the band-coloured ring between them (8.87:1 against the fill, computed from the tokens); the keyboard ring is the fill colour separated by a band-coloured gap. 41 real Tab presses through the workbench chrome reach the one stop. Flows drive a click on the track, a drag of the thumb, the arrow, page, Home and End keys, the wheel with and without `wheelUnits`, both thumbs of a range, a grab press, the label action and an off-centre hover through the pointer; the screen-reader value is read from source: the AT-SPI bus is not available in the slot.
