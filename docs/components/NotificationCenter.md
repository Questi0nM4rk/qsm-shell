# NotificationCenter

A searchable history of notifications grouped by application; it filters, groups and navigates, and the host owns dismissal and every consequence.
states: Unread and All views (Unread keeps a row read during the visit until the view changes or `beginVisit()`), list and the three empty states (`All caught up`, `Nothing here`, `No matches`); do-not-disturb on (a translucent accent tint over the band and an accent glyph, through `IconButton.selected`); Clear armed (the glyph becomes a tick in `danger` and the name says confirm, so the state is not colour alone)
look: the search field takes the whole first row (a menu rolling out of a screen corner opens under the pointer, and a field is the one control a click there cannot harm); the view switch and the two icon buttons share the second row, 40 px each, 8 px apart; groups are headed by the application's name, so a card in a group does not repeat it (`showAppName: false`); entries sort by group (live first, most recent first), live before archived within it, equal-recency groups keep feed order
actions: `focusField(fromPointer)`, `moveSelection`, `toggleSelected`, `activateSelected`, `removeSelected` and `moveAction` are the keyboard route and the programmatic one; hover, dismiss, action and activation intents report upward and never resolve inside. `entries` are copied before sorting; an archived record's identity is `archiveId`, else its timestamps and text
pointer: hovering a row moves the cursor while `Interaction.pointerSelects`; the leading column of a row opens it (and nothing else), a body click on an unread row reports `activated`, the dismiss button, middle click and swipe of a live row report `dismissRequested`; the wheel always scrolls the list and no row takes it
keyboard: the search field holds the focus and routes Up and Down to the selection (`selectionClaimed`), Enter to `activateSelected` (the focused action, else the row's expansion), Escape to `escaped`; every other key reports first through `keyPressed`, so the host can claim Delete or Tab (the unit does what the desktop does: Delete removes the selected row)
screen reader: the list is named `Notifications`, rows are named `<app>: <summary>` with "Unread." when unread, the do-not-disturb button is a checkable button, the view switch is named `Notification views`, the clear button's name flips to `Confirm clear history`
size: the list fills what the host gives and never shrinks to its content (a list that shrank re-centred the header); its natural height is content up to `maxListHeight` with a floor of two collapsed rows; the cards reach `Style.cardShadowExtent` past the list on each side so their shadows are not clipped
tokens: space scale, iconMd, textMuted, danger, accent, `opacitySelected`, notificationCollapsedHeight, TextField, Segmented, IconButton, ArmedAction, ScrollCue, EmptyState; `fadeColor` is required, the host states its ground
motion: none of its own; cards and cues settle their own changes
measured: in the unit the header rows are 40 px with 8 px between (search 16 to 56, controls 64 to 104), the list starts 12 px below (116), a group heading is 16 px tall and sits 16 px under the card above and 4 px over its own, the cards are as wide as the search field (404) and 8 px apart; a card is 72 px with a one-line body and 92 with two. The do-not-disturb disc renders #3C3046 on #271E30 and its accent glyph reads 6.85:1 on it
expected: an Enter on an unread row expands it and does not mark it read, as the leading-column click does; marking a row read from the keyboard is the host's (`activated`) until the owner rules otherwise
not designed: live ephemeral display, swipe physics or dismissal itself; use NotificationStack, NotificationCard or the host for those
