# Dialog

A modal takeover that dims the ground, traps focus and returns it on close; the surface that opened it owns dismissal policy.
states: open and closed; open fades and settles the card in (`Motion.medium`, the settle scale `Motion.microPopScale`), close fades it out in `Motion.exit`; the card keeps its shape while invisible so layout never jumps; reduced motion keeps the fade and drops the settle.
actions: slotted controls in the default `actions` slot, declared in reading order (the first takes focus, the last trails); Escape and the scrim only report `dismissRequested`, and close restores focus and emits `closed`. Pass `returnFocusTo` a control that survives the action, because an invoker that is removed or hidden refuses focus and Dialog has no fallback.
pointer: the scrim catches hover, click and wheel of every button so nothing reaches the surface behind; the card absorbs presses and the wheel on its text and padding so they never dismiss; the body scrolls under a fixed title and actions; during the fade out a shield catches the pointer.
keyboard: focus moves to the first action (the card itself with no action) on open, even when a scrolling body stands ahead of it, and back to the captured item on close; head and tail sentinels trap Tab and Shift+Tab inside, through the scrolling body when it overflows; Escape reports only while open, from any control or the body.
screen reader: the dialog is `Accessible.Dialog` named by `title` and described by `body`; slotted actions name themselves; the scrim is ignored.
size: `dialogWidth`, never wider than the window less `space6` a side; height follows content up to the window less `space6` a side, then the body scrolls; one `space5` inset, `space3` between the title row and the body, `space5` before the actions; position and height land on whole device pixels. `tone` is `neutral`, `danger` or `warning`; anything else logs an error and draws neutral.
tokens: controlWidth, space5, space3, space2, iconMd, radiusXl, overlay, accent, danger, warning, textMd for the body, microPopScale and Prose; no local colours or measurements.
motion: medium opacity and settle, emphasized entry and exit curves; reduced motion removes the settle.
expected: shell Workbench → Dialog: the HDMI row's remove action opens it over the dimmed output list; Keep takes focus, Tab cycles Keep, Remove (and the body when it scrolls), Escape and the dim report a dismissal that the host may refuse, and focus lands back on the invoker.
not designed: modeless popovers, inline messages, form fields or dismissal policy; use Popover, Callout, Field or the host for those.

Measured (default theme, 1.25 scale, headless window): the card measured 380 by 167.7 at y 166.15 (209.6 and 207.7 device px, both fractional) and now lands on 380 by 229.6 at y 135.2 (287 and 169 device px; the body reads at 14 px instead of 12, three lines); the gap before the actions was 20 and is 24; insets 24, 24, 24, 23.6 (the device grid); Keep and Remove are 40 high, 8 apart; title 8.02:1, body 5.03:1, the Remove label 5.17:1 and the tone mark 5.17:1 on the overlay; open settles 90 % in about 130 ms and the exit takes 180 ms at 60 fps; at a 320 px window the card is 256 wide and the actions stay in reach.
